Support

SafeNow is an emergency guide you keep on your phone. It is not a rescue service.

If you are in danger now

Follow local officials. Use the emergency number published for the country or area you are in. The SOS screen in SafeNow is a light and sound on this phone only. It does not place a call and it does not contact emergency services. This website cannot see where you are.

How to get help with the app

Open a GitHub Issue on the public SafeNow site repository. Please do not include medical details, exact home addresses, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Open a support issue

You can also browse existing issues.

Using the app

  1. Open SafeNow once while you still have a connection, so the bundled guides and your settings can finish loading.
  2. The app is designed to work offline after that first launch. The compass, saved places, checklists, and guides are meant to stay available without internet, Wi-Fi, or cell service. GPS can still work from satellites.
  3. If you want nearby OpenStreetMap places or a small walking-route map, save them while you are online. Sample places are marked SAMPLE and are not official shelters.

Location

Location is used on the device to point the compass and to show coordinates on the SOS screen. If you deny location access, type a latitude and longitude or search a city you know, then save places while online. See the privacy policy.

This version does not take payment

There is no account, no in-app purchase, and no billing in this version. Safety features are not locked.
