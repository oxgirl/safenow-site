On the phone

Tools that stay with you

Guides, places you save, and tools stay on the device. There is no account and no SafeNow server for your notes.

Places

Checklists

Guides

SOS, on this phone only

Shelter compass

A heading toward a place you actually have

A large arrow points toward a place you saved, with the distance and a walking time. It uses this phone’s location and motion sensors on the device. A street map is not required.

SafeNow does not invent shelters. Places come from official lists you load, OpenStreetMap data you save, or places you enter yourself. If none are loaded, the app says that no verified data is available.

Checklists

What to pack, kept on this phone

Go-bag lists based on the Ready.gov emergency kit and the Red Cross supply list. Check items on the phone. They stay here with no account.

Survival guide

Before, during, and after

Emergency guides are split into short stages: what to prepare, what to do while it is happening, and what to do after. Follow official instructions if they differ from the app.

Twenty languages

Written to be used anywhere

SafeNow is being built in 20 languages. Text in languages other than English, including first-aid steps, was translated with machine assistance and still needs review by a native speaker.

Also on the phone

More tools, same rules

Each of these stays on the device. None of them contact emergency services for you.

First aid, including CPR

Short topics such as CPR, bleeding, burns, and choking. Text in languages other than English, including first-aid steps, was translated with machine assistance and still needs review by a native speaker. This is reference guidance, not a substitute for training or official care.

SOS is local only

The SOS screen can flash the screen, play an optional alarm, and use the torch if the phone allows it. It does not place a call and does not contact emergency services.

Family medical card

Store blood type, allergies, and other notes on the device. There is no account and no SafeNow server for this card.

Supply tracker

A supplies list you keep on the phone, so you can see what you already have on hand.

Battery saver

A mode that switches the app to a darker screen, to reduce what the display is doing.

Readiness check

A checklist that shows whether guides are in the app, whether a real shelter is saved, and whether a family plan has been started. It is a reminder on this phone, not a certification that the app has been tested on a physical device in airplane mode.

Phrase cards

Short cards for asking for help, water, a doctor, shelter, and family, in the language you choose.

Optional walking map

If you want a small street map or walking streets, save an area while you are online. Map tiles and nearby OpenStreetMap places are fetched only when you ask. Community map data can be incomplete.

Safety disclaimer

The SOS screen is a light and sound on this phone only. SafeNow does not place a call and does not contact emergency services. If you are in danger, use the official emergency number for the area you are in.

SafeNow does not replace official warnings. If authorities tell you to do something else, do that.

SafeNow does not invent shelters. Community map data from OpenStreetMap can be incomplete. Load a real list from your community before a crisis when you can. If no official list is loaded, the app says that no verified data is available.

Guide text in languages other than English was translated with machine assistance and still needs review by a native speaker. First-aid pages are reference guidance, not a substitute for training or professional care.

Payment

No account and no in-app purchases

There is no account, no in-app purchase, and no billing. Every safety feature stays open.

Privacy

A simple privacy promise

There is no account, and there is no SafeNow server for your notes. Read the full privacy policy.

No account, no analytics

SafeNow has no login. It does not include an analytics, advertising, crash, or tracking SDK. It does not sell personal information.

Location stays on the phone

The compass uses this device’s location and motion sensors on the device. That location is not sent to SafeNow.

Your notes stay here

Places, family notes, the medical card, checklist ticks, language, and settings are stored on the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site data removes them.

Network only when you ask

City search, nearby map data, and street-map tiles go to public OpenStreetMap services only if you request them while you are online.

Questions

Questions

Does SafeNow contact emergency services?

No. The SOS screen is a light and sound on this phone only. It does not place a call and it does not contact emergency services. If you are in danger, use the official emergency number for the area you are in.

Are there in-app purchases?

There is no account, no in-app purchase, and no billing. Safety features are not locked.

When will it be on the App Store and Google Play?

App Store: coming soon. SafeNow has been submitted for App Store review and is not yet available to download on the App Store or Google Play.

Where do the shelters come from?

SafeNow does not invent shelters. Places come from official lists you load, OpenStreetMap data you save, or places you enter yourself. If none are loaded, the app says that no verified data is available. Community map data can be incomplete.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no login. Places, family notes, medical card details, and checklist ticks stay on the device. See the privacy policy.

Where does the guidance come from?

Guidance is drawn from Ready.gov, FEMA, CDC, the American Red Cross, and public-domain U.S. Army survival manuals. Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. SafeNow does not replace official warnings.

Are the translations finished?

The app is being built in 20 languages. Text in languages other than English, including first-aid steps, was translated with machine assistance and still needs review by a native speaker.

How do I get help with the app?

See the support page. You can open a GitHub Issue on the public SafeNow site repository. Please do not include medical details, exact home addresses, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
