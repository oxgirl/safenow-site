Emergency app

Get to a saved shelter when the network is down.

SafeNow is a free, politically neutral emergency app. Its aim is to help people survive, not to make money. This version does not take payment.

It includes a shelter compass, checklists, a survival guide, first aid, a family medical card, a supplies list, a battery saver, and a readiness check. It is available in 20 languages.

The app is designed to work offline after you open it once with a connection. Guides and your saved places stay on the phone. A street map is optional and is only fetched when you ask for one while online.

SOS is local only

The SOS screen is a light and sound on this phone only. SafeNow does not contact emergency services. If you are in danger, use the official emergency number where you are.

On this phone only

There is no account and no SafeNow server for your places, family notes, or checklist ticks. Location is used on the device to point the compass and to show coordinates on the SOS screen. See the privacy policy for what happens if you download shelter data or a map while online.

SafeNow does not replace official warnings. If authorities tell you to do something else, do that.
